Trainer Guidance

TRAINER QUESTIONS & ADVICE

Answers to common questions about selecting the ideal trainer for your specific fitness goals.

COACH MATCHING How does Stackly match me with the ideal personal coach?

During your complimentary 45-minute consultation, our coaching director evaluates your movement mechanics, training history, schedule availability, and primary targets (e.g., rapid fat loss, hypertrophy, Olympic lifting, joint rehab). We pair you directly with the master coach whose domain specialty best aligns with your biological baseline.

InBody 570 Biometrics Screen Movement Quality Audit Guaranteed Domain Match
PROGRAM PIVOT Can I switch trainers or goals as my fitness progresses?

Absolutely. We operate a fully unified coaching methodology. If your career schedule changes or you transition from metabolic conditioning to competitive Olympic weightlifting, we seamlessly transfer your complete workout telemetry, progressive overload logs, and biometric scans to your new specialized coach.

Zero Retraining Delay Full History Cloud Sync Flexible Scheduling
FIRST SESSION What happens during my very first 1-on-1 coaching session?

Your first session begins with a multi-frequency InBody 570 body composition scan, followed by a joint mobility screening and a tailored introductory working set where your coach dials in baseline working weights, bar speed, and observes kinetic movement mechanics.

Segmental Muscle Mass Scan Joint Clearance Check Custom Working Weight Map
MACRONUTRITION Do master coaches provide personalized diet & macro programming?

Yes. Training without precise fueling yields sub-optimal muscle gain. Every 1-on-1 package includes custom daily calorie & macronutrient targets (protein, carbs, fats), peri-workout fueling timing, hydration benchmarks, and weekly diet check-ins.

Daily Protein Benchmarks Peri-Workout Hydration Weekly App Auditing
FREQUENCY How many sessions per week deliver the fastest results?

Most Stackly athletes achieve breakthrough results with 2 to 3 coached sessions per week paired with 1 or 2 autonomous recovery or cardio days. Your master coach will engineer an exact periodized weekly split tailored to your recovery capacity.

Periodized 12-Week Blocks Built-in Deload Weeks Autonomous Homework Splits
