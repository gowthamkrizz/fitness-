Have Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining Stackly, our cancellation flexibility, and member privileges.

Can I cancel or freeze my membership anytime?

Yes. Stackly memberships operate on a hassle-free basis. You can freeze your membership for up to 60 days per calendar year directly in the member portal, or cancel with 14 days notice before your next billing cycle with zero penalties.

What is included in the 7-day free trial?

Your 7-day free trial gives you complete 24/7 facility access, an InBody 570 body scan, one 45-minute 1-on-1 personal training consultation, and unlimited access to all coach-led group classes.

How do personal trainer session bookings work?

Members on Pro Athlete or Elite VIP tiers can view coach schedules and reserve 1-on-1 slots up to 14 days in advance directly through our Stackly Member Portal or mobile app.

Can I bring a guest to workout with me?

Pro Athlete members receive 1 complimentary guest day pass per month, and Elite VIP members receive 4 passes per month. Additional day passes are available at a discounted rate of $15.

Do you offer student, military, or couple discounts?

Yes! We proudly offer a 15% discount on monthly memberships for active military, first responders, healthcare workers, full-time students, and couples signing up together. Contact front desk support to apply your discount.
