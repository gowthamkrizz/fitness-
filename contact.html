Before You Visit

VISITING STACKLY: WHAT TO EXPECT

Key guidelines and amenities to ensure your first visit is completely seamless.

What should I bring for my first consultation?

Please wear standard athletic training attire and gym sneakers. We provide complimentary digital lockers, chilled water stations, and towel service. We recommend drinking 500ml of water 1 hour prior to your InBody scan for peak measurement accuracy.

Is parking available at the facility?

Yes. We provide 2 hours of complimentary validated parking in our dedicated underground garage beneath the Manhattan campus, with valet service available during prime morning and evening training hours.

Can I try out the recovery spa after my workout?

Absolutely! All trial consultation guests are warmly invited to experience our Finnish cedar dry sauna, 45°F cold plunge pools, and luxury rain showers immediately following their coaching evaluation.

What if I have previous injuries or joint limitations?

Our master coaches are CSCS and FMS certified specialists. During your initial movement screen, your coach will identify any mobility restrictions or past injuries and tailor every exercise to protect and rehabilitate your joints.
